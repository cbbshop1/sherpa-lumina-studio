## Changes

- Replace the long landing page with six dashboard cards and focused routes.
- Keep a shared selection and preview transport across learning workspaces.
- Show selected chord/scale notes before playback and after stopping.
- Carry selections into Pitch & Tone and into the existing piano's highlight props.
- Share one favorites/progressions state owner and preserve the existing storage keys and data structure. Load saved state before writing; keep invalid saved data intact and report the limitation.
- Route every preview voice through the analyser from the first playback.
- Cancel future strum voices and pending resumes on Stop; retain ownership of fading voices until they end.
- Retain true octave extensions when sounding chords instead of folding them into one octave.
- Add waveform/spectrum views and common tone controls. Piano and sequencer continue to use their existing instruments.
- Apply a dark green, parchment, peach, and sage studio palette with serif headings, compact cards, clear focus states, and reduced-motion support.
